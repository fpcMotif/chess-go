Under the castle, a board waits. Win, and the door opens.

Past the trapdoor is a chessboard as big as a hall. Its pieces stand taller than you, and they move when you tell them to. Play that game in 3D, in your browser.

plate i · the pieces that guard the door

Every piece is alive

Click a piece, then a square, and it goes where you send it. The gold army is amber and ruby glass. The frost army is sapphire. The knights are winged horses. Each piece glows from inside.

plate ii · who plays the other side

Choose who moves the frost army

The board needs someone across from you. Three of the five are Stockfish 19 at different strengths. One is your friend on the next chair. One leaves both sides to the engine while you watch.

plate iii · ember field

On this board, a taken piece is smashed

Enchanted chess is not polite. The taking piece lifts, leaps, and crashes down. The taken piece bursts into embers, and a shockwave runs across the board.

  1. Lift The piece rises off its square.
  2. Leap It arcs over the board.
  3. Smash It lands on the taken piece.
  4. Ember burst The taken piece burns away.
